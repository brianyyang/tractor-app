- [x] Ending games and calculating
      At the end of each game, we want a total of the points scored for each person. Total points is the sum of the difference between a player and every other player.

Example: susan is 10, sisi is 11, penny is 12
penny wins 2 points from susan and 1 point from susan
sisi wins 1 point from susan and loses 1 point from penny
susan loses 2 points from penny and 1 point from sisi
